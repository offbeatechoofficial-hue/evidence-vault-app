Legal Guide

How cyberbullying evidence can be documented and reported

Recommended Steps

1. Preserve harmful messages, screenshots, usernames, links, and timestamps.

2. Do not edit or crop screenshots. Keep original evidence whenever possible.

3. Record repeated threats, harassment, stalking, impersonation, or blackmail incidents.

4. Report abuse to the platform where it occurred.

5. If serious threats or extortion are involved, contact local law enforcement or cybercrime authorities.

6. Use generated reports from this system as organized supporting documentation.


Important Note

This application is an academic/demo tool for secure evidence organization. Legal admissibility depends on jurisdiction, chain of custody, and official procedures.
